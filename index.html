The digits at a glance

Lucky, unlucky and “it depends”

Chinese number luck is mostly about sound. A digit is lucky when it sounds like a good word and unlucky when it sounds like a bad one. That’s why 8 (sounds like “prosper”) is everywhere, and 4 (sounds like “death”) goes missing from lift buttons.

FAQ

What is the luckiest number in Chinese culture?

8 (bā), because it sounds like 发 (fā), “to prosper”. 6 (smooth) and 9 (long-lasting) are close behind.

Why do Chinese people avoid the number 4?

4 (sì) sounds almost identical to 死 (sǐ), “death”. Many buildings in China skip floors 4, 14 and 24.

What does 520 mean?

520 (wǔ èr líng) sounds like 我爱你 (wǒ ài nǐ), “I love you”. May 20 has become an unofficial online Valentine’s Day in China.
