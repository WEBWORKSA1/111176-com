FAQ

Why does my zodiac sign differ from websites that use the year only?

The Chinese zodiac year starts at Lunar New Year (between 21 January and 20 February), not 1 January. If you were born in January or early February you may belong to the previous year’s sign. Our finder uses the Chinese calendar built into your browser.

Are zodiac lucky numbers the same everywhere?

No. Traditional sources differ slightly. We list the most commonly cited digits for each sign.
