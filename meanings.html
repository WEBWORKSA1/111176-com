FAQ

What does 1314 mean in Chinese?

1314 (yī sān yī sì) sounds like 一生一世 (yì shēng yí shì), “one life, one world” — forever. 5201314 means “I love you forever”.

What does 666 mean?

666 (liù liù liù) is internet slang for “awesome” or “skilful”. It comes from 溜 (liù), meaning smooth or slick. Unlike in the West, it is positive.

What does 88 mean in Chinese texting?

88 (bā bā) sounds like “bye-bye”. It also means double prosperity.

What does 250 mean?

二百五 (èr bǎi wǔ) is an insult meaning idiot or fool. Avoid it in prices and gifts.
